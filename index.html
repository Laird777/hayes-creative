About

Hi, I'm Nicki.

I'm a digital marketer who plans content, makes it, and measures it. I design the graphics, write the copy, then check the numbers to see what worked.

Through my Digital Marketing Apprenticeship with Acadium I ran social media for Tealway ABA and led SEO strategy, local landing pages and blog content for Luqsee. As a volunteer social media coordinator for Mel's Farm Animal Sanctuary, I also ran an adoption campaign that people shared hundreds of times.

Before marketing, I trained in theatre at Canadore College and spent years in customer-facing roles. That's where I learned to understand what people actually need. I'm looking for a role in social media, content or SEO where the work is measured and the audience matters. The work I'm proudest of is useful to someone: a parent looking for a calmer home, or a dog who needed people to hit share.
