Shyla needed a home. People shared her story.

Mel's Farm Animal Sanctuary · Foster & adoption awareness · Facebook

My role: Volunteer marketing campaigner and social media coordinator. I designed the campaign graphics and wrote the messaging for both posts.

Shyla, a 4-year-old black German Shepherd, needed a foster or forever home. I made two story-driven Facebook posts designed to be shared beyond the sanctuary's own followers. Together they were shared 419 times, and Shyla found a home.

539 Reactions, comments and shares

Challenge

One dog, a specific need, a limited audience

Shyla needed a particular kind of home: quiet, in the country, with a safe, fenced yard. To find the right person, her story had to travel well beyond the sanctuary's existing audience.

Goal

Awareness that spreads

Create emotionally engaging content that raises awareness of Shyla and gets the community to share her foster and adoption story.

Strategy

Design for the share, not the like

A like keeps a post inside the sanctuary's audience. A share puts it in front of someone new, who might be the one. So I optimized the creative for sharing:

  • Lead with Shyla, not the sanctuary. Her face and personality do the work. People share a dog they feel for.
  • Name the need plainly. A quiet country home with a fenced yard, so the right person recognizes themselves and everyone else knows who to share it with.
  • End with a clear emotional call to action: “Could you be the one she's been waiting for?”
Execution

Two posts, two angles

The first post told Shyla's story and her need. The second reframed it as a direct, specific ask for a forever foster home.

Results

People passed it on, and Shyla found a home.

Shares made up most of the engagement on both posts: 302 of 373 interactions on the first, 117 of 166 on the second. The audience didn't just engage with Shyla's story. They actively helped spread it, and Shyla found a home.

302 Shares · Post 1 (plus 64 reactions, 7 comments)
